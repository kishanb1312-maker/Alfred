# Sources

All figures in this dossier were gathered via web research on **10 September 2026**. Secondary
sources vary in rigour — where two sources disagreed (e.g. WordPress share reported as 40.7% vs
41.5% vs 43%), the range is noted in the text rather than a single number being asserted.

**Nothing here is primary research.** No user interviews have been conducted. The personas,
journey maps and JTBD in `03-user-research-and-personas.md` are *hypotheses built from secondary
sources and market patterns* and must be validated. That document contains the research plan to
do so, including explicit kill criteria.
